# Twelve-week DSA roadmap

**Start:** 12 October 2026. Plan for roughly 4–5 focused DSA hours each week, alongside university coursework, Japanese study and ITEE preparation. Problem counts are goals, not quotas.

| Week | Dates | Pattern(s) | Practice target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 12–18 | Arrays, hash sets and dictionaries | Contains Duplicate, Valid Anagram, Two Sum |
| 2 | Oct 19–25 | Hashing review, frequency maps | Ransom Note, Majority Element, revisit Two Sum |
| 3 | Oct 26–Nov 1 | Two pointers | Valid Palindrome, Reverse String, Move Zeroes |
| 4 | Nov 2–8 | Stack and basic recursion | Valid Parentheses, Baseball Game |
| 5 | Nov 9–15 | Binary search | Binary Search, Search Insert Position, Sqrt(x) |
| 6 | Nov 16–22 | Sliding window | Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock, Maximum Average Subarray I |
| 7 | Nov 23–29 | Linked lists | Merge Two Sorted Lists, Linked List Cycle, Middle of the Linked List |
| 8 | Nov 30–Dec 6 | Introductory trees | Maximum Depth, Same Tree, Invert Binary Tree |
| 9 | Dec 7–13 | Simple dynamic programming | Climbing Stairs, Fibonacci Number |
| 10 | Dec 14–20 | Graph introduction | Flood Fill; Number of Islands (stretch) |
| 11 | Dec 21–27 | Mixed pattern review | Revisit unsolved/weak problems, Group Anagrams (stretch) |
| 12 | Dec 28–Jan 3 | Timed revision | Re-solve Two Sum, Binary Search, one unfamiliar easy problem |

**How to study each problem:** 20–25 minute attempt → hint if necessary → independent reimplementation → edge-case tests → complexity note → revisit in 3–7 days.

**Checkpoint after 12 weeks:** aim to confidently re-solve at least 20 problems and explain why the solutions work. Reduce the weekly target during final examinations and relocation.
